Book Nest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

App: Book Nest (Android package io.booknest.app) Developer: Mohamed Gomaa Contact: MohamedGomaaRadwan@gmail.com

Book Nest is a personal library for books you already own. It works without an account and keeps your library on your device. The same policy is shown in the app (Settings → About → Privacy policy) in 14 languages.

What stays on your device

Your books, covers, notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading progress and goals, memorization passages, flashcards, study subjects, questions, exam results, plans and settings are stored only on your device, in Book Nest's private storage. The developer never receives them.

Internet and network use

Book Nest doesn't need the internet. It has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The only network use is moving your library to another device on the same Wi-Fi, which you start yourself and protect with a one-time code. Links in your books open in your browser only when you choose to. The app includes Google's Firebase Core library, which by itself sends no usage data.

Permissions

  • Notifications: reminders you turn on.
  • Microphone: only while you recite a passage aloud, if you use that. Your words are turned into text by your phone's speech service (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone); when on-device recognition isn't available, that service may process the audio on its own servers under its own privacy policy.
  • Fingerprint or face: only if you lock the app or private books; Book Nest never sees your biometric data.
  • Files: Book Nest uses the system file picker, so it doesn't ask for access to all your files.

On-device AI

On supported iPhones and iPads, optional features use Apple's on-device model. Text you send to it stays on the device.

Backups and sharing

Backups and library files are created only when you ask, saved on your device, and shared only where you choose. Book Nest doesn't upload them anywhere.

On Android, Book Nest turns off automatic system backup, so your library isn't copied to the cloud; use Backup & restore instead. On iPhone and iPad, the device's own backup (iCloud or a computer) can include Book Nest's data, as it does for other apps.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete books and notes in the app, clear the search index and cache in Settings → Storage, or remove everything by uninstalling Book Nest. Backups you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Children

Book Nest doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
